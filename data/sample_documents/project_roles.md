# Project Roles

Project Manager: owns overall delivery, runs weekly progress meetings and escalates high-risk schedule items.

Design Manager: coordinates architectural and engineering design, controls design changes and supports procurement decisions.

Procurement Manager: tracks supplier milestones, manages purchase orders and escalates long-lead risks.

MEP Coordinator: coordinates electrical, HVAC and plumbing activities, leads coordination reviews and tracks installation conflicts.

QA/QC Manager: maintains quality checklists, coordinates inspections and tracks corrective actions.
