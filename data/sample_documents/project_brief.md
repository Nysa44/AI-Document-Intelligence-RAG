# Aurora Office Renovation — Project Brief

The Aurora Office Renovation will modernize a 12,000 square-foot office facility. Scope includes architectural updates, electrical upgrades, HVAC improvements, interior finishes, testing and handover.

The planned project duration is 24 weeks.

Major phases:
1. Design — Weeks 1–4
2. Procurement — Weeks 3–8
3. Demolition — Weeks 6–9
4. MEP installation — Weeks 8–16
5. Interior finishes — Weeks 14–21
6. Testing and commissioning — Weeks 21–23
7. Handover — Week 24

Major schedule risks include procurement lead times, MEP trade coordination and late design changes.

The project manager conducts a weekly progress meeting every Monday. High-risk schedule items must be escalated within two business days.

The approved project budget is $2.8 million. Procurement packages above $100,000 require commercial approval.
